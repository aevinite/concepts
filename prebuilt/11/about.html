About Aevinite

Aevinite is a company based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. It was founded on 28 July 2024 by Rishi Nakrani (Founder) and Jenish Patel (Co-Founder) under the name Infinite, and became Aevinite on 16 June 2026. We help businesses grow, with websites, software and automation built around how each business works. Our own product, Aevidine, is restaurant software.

What the name means

Aevinite is derived from Aevum, a dimension of existence with a beginning but no end, and it echoes Infinite, the name the company started with. Hence the tagline: The beginning with no end.

Frequently asked questions

What is Aevinite?
Aevinite is a company based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India that helps businesses grow, with websites, software and automation built around how each business works. It also makes Aevidine, restaurant software.
Who founded Aevinite?
Aevinite was founded by Rishi Nakrani (Founder) and Jenish Patel (Co-Founder).
When was Aevinite founded?
Aevinite was founded on 28 July 2024 in Ahmedabad under the name Infinite, and was renamed Aevinite on 16 June 2026.
Where is Aevinite based?

What does the name Aevinite mean?
Aevinite is derived from Aevum, a dimension of existence with a beginning but no end, and echoes the word Infinite, the company's original name. The name means "the beginning with no end"; the company's line is "We help businesses grow."
What has Aevinite built?
Aevinite's work includes Aevidine, a complete restaurant platform with QR ordering, kitchen screens, billing and owner reports; a multispeciality hospital website; the 4D Dish Explorer, an interactive 3D dish viewer; HackJudge, a hackathon judging platform; and scroll-driven 3D brand showcases.
