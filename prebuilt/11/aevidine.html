Our product

AevidineRestaurant software for QR ordering, KOT and billing

Aevidine is restaurant management software made by Aevinite in Ahmedabad. Guests order from a QR code menu at the table, the kitchen gets every order as a KOT, and waiters, managers and the owner run tables, bills and reports from their own screens. It works on any phone, tablet or computer, and keeps working when the internet drops.

How it works

One system, a screen for everyone

Guests

QR code menu and ordering

Guests scan the QR code on their table and order from their phone. No app to download: the menu opens in the browser, with categories, search, veg and non-veg filters, and a page for every dish with photos, price and nutrition.

Kitchen

Kitchen screen and KOT printing

Every order reaches the kitchen the moment it is placed, as a KOT (kitchen order ticket) on the kitchen screen and on the kitchen printer, so nothing is lost between the table and the cook.

Waiters

Waiter tablet

Waiters take orders at the table, see which tables are busy, move orders and close bills from a tablet or phone.

Managers

Manager panel

Managers edit the menu, prices and categories, and run the floor and the day's bills.

Owner

Owner reports

The owner sees sales, bills and how the day is going, from anywhere, with daily reports and GST summaries.

Watch

What the guest sees

The guest scans the QR sticker, browses the menu with search and filters, opens each dish with its allergens, sees it in 3D, and orders; the allergy note reaches the kitchen ticket. Six languages.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Aevidine?
Aevidine is restaurant management software. Guests order from a QR code menu at the table; the kitchen receives each order as a KOT on a screen and a printer; waiters, managers and the owner run tables, bills and reports from their own screens.
Who makes Aevidine?
Aevidine is made by Aevinite, a company that helps businesses grow, based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India, founded by Rishi Nakrani and Jenish Patel.
Do guests need to download an app?
No. Guests scan the QR code on the table and the menu opens in their phone's browser.
Does Aevidine print KOTs and bills?
Yes. Orders print as KOTs (kitchen order tickets) in the kitchen, and bills and GST invoices print at the counter.
What happens if the internet goes down?
The screens keep working. Orders taken while offline are saved and sent as soon as the connection is back.
Where is Aevidine available?
Aevidine is built in Ahmedabad and is available to restaurants, cafés and bakeries across India.
