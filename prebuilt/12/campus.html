Our product

Campus ConveyanceThe student transport app for schools and colleges

Every morning, every parent in the city asks the same question: where is the bus? Campus Conveyance, made by Aevinite in Ahmedabad, answers it. Students and parents book a seat and pay by UPI, the driver's phone becomes the live bus, a QR scan at the door tells the family the child is on board, and the college, the transport agency and the admin each see their part.

Who it's for

Six people. One ride.

How it works

Five panels, one system.

Students and parents01

Book, pay, track

Pick the college, the route and the stop, choose a seat and pay by UPI. The pass is a QR code on the phone. Parents can book for more than one child, and get "your bus is 10 minutes away".

Drivers02

The phone becomes the bus

The driver goes online and the bus shows up live on every map. At the door, the driver scans each pass, and the family is told the child is on board.

Schools and colleges03

Every bus coming in

The college sees every bus on its way to campus, with its riders and routes, without calling anyone.

Transport agencies04

The whole fleet on one screen

Routes, buses, drivers, riders, bookings, refunds and revenue. The agency assigns each rider to a bus and can move a route to another bus.

Admin05

Every payment, checked

Each UPI payment is checked before a seat is confirmed, and an agency's badge appears only after its proof is checked.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Campus Conveyance?
Campus Conveyance is a student transport app. Students and parents book a seat on a school or college bus and pay by UPI, watch the bus live on a map, and are told when the child boards. Drivers, colleges, transport agencies and an admin each have their own panel.
Who makes Campus Conveyance?
Campus Conveyance is made by Aevinite, a company that helps businesses grow, based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India, founded by Rishi Nakrani and Jenish Patel. Aevinite also makes Aevidine, restaurant software.
How do parents know the child is on the bus?
Every rider has a QR pass. The driver scans it at the bus door, and the parent gets a notice on their phone at that moment.
Does the bus need a GPS device?
No. The driver's phone shares the bus's live location while the driver is online, so there is no hardware to buy or fit.
How do students pay?
By UPI, inside the app. The payment is checked before the seat is confirmed, and refunds are handled by the transport agency.
Is Campus Conveyance available now? Can I see a demo?
It runs on the web and on Android, and is ready for its first schools, colleges and transport agencies in Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar. For a free demo, email rishi@aevinite.com or call or WhatsApp +91 90997 23703.
